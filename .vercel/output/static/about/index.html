About TD

We're on a mission to democratize frontend development education and help developers reach their full potential.

Our Mission

TD was founded with a simple vision: to make high-quality frontend development education accessible to everyone. We believe that great developers aren't born—they're trained through structured learning, practical experience, and community support.

Our platform combines carefully curated content, hands-on projects, and a vibrant community to help developers at every level master the skills they need to succeed in today's job market.

Our Story

2020

The Beginning

TD was born out of frustration with fragmented learning resources. Our founder spent months researching the best way to teach frontend development and realized there was a gap in the market for structured, practical education.

2021

First Cohort

We launched with 100 beta users and iterated based on their feedback. The response was incredible—85% completion rate and overwhelmingly positive testimonials. We knew we were onto something special.

2022

Rapid Growth

We expanded our team, added new courses (Angular and Vue), and hit 10,000 learners. The community became the heart of TD, with peer learning and mentorship emerging as key success factors.

Today

Empowering Developers Worldwide

With 50,000+ active learners from 150+ countries, we're proud to be a trusted platform for frontend development education. Our commitment to quality, practical learning remains as strong as ever.
